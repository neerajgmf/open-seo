# Competitor Landscape — List & Inspiration Index

The durable reference for competitor names and inspiration URLs. Use this when designing a page
to decide which competitors/pages to study. Per-page competitor teardowns are NOT kept here —
they live in that page's `work-log/{task-name}/DECISIONS-AND-AGENT-CONVERSATIONS.md` working notes.

Sources: "Product Inspirations and Competitors" list, the "Landing pages - GoStudio2.0 - Content"
planning CSV, and the founder competitor set supplied 2026-08-19. Cross-referenced with the
Competitive Positioning section of
`skills/gostudio-product-marketing-context/references/product-marketing-context.md`.

> **Framing (updated 2026-08-19).** The competitive threat is **specialised simplicity**, not
> model aggregation. GoStudio's buyer is not shopping for frontier models — they want one job
> done well. The battle is against tools that do one job simply (Photoroom, Mokker, Botika), with
> the wedge sitting between **too general** (ChatGPT/Gemini) and **too complex**
> (Higgsfield, Freepik/Magnific). Model aggregators are context, not the daily fight.

## Reference Index — pages to study per competitor

URLs to teardown when building the matching GoStudio page.

**PhotoRoom** — tool landing pages: product-staging, virtual-model, ghost-mannequin, flat-lay,
remove-wrinkles-clothes, edit-with-ai (editor), recolor, video-generator. Footer = automation
inspiration. CSV note: "Botika is better" than PhotoRoom's virtual-model.

**Botika** — solutions: launch-campaigns-faster, increase-diversity, get-to-market-faster,
cut-production-costs. Products: on-model, mannequin, flat-lay, ai-fashion-video,
ai-fashion-models. CSV note: GoStudio product pages should "give the details like Botika."

**Soona** — models (foot/hand/pet/full-body), product-photography categories (home-goods,
beauty, pet, shopify, etsy + more), tools/integrations. Footer = inspiration.

**SellerPic** — template/inspiration landing pages (category-level); print-on-demand page.

**Creatify.ai** — template gallery structure (UGC / TikTok / Commercial tabs), template naming
convention, card layout. Teardown captured in `work-log/marketing-studio/`.

**Flair.ai** — fashion prompt/category community pages (community.flair.ai/explore).

**Picsart / Magnific** — homepage + many-model playground inspiration.
