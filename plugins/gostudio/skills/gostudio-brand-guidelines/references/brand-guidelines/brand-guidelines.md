## Introduction

The GoStudio.ai brand guidelines provide a framework for representing the brand identity consistently across touchpoints. The guidelines are intended to help GoStudio.ai communicate its values, personality, and offerings with consistency and coherence.

The guidelines support audience trust, brand recognition, and a strong brand presence.

## About the Brand

GoStudio.ai is described in the guideline PDF as an AI-generated professional headshot and photo-editing site that specializes in creating multiple headshots for different purposes.

For current product positioning, use `../../../gostudio-product-marketing-context/references/product-marketing-context.md` as the higher-priority product and copy source. **Do not rely on any positioning line quoted in this brand file — pull it live from that source.** As of 2026-08-19 the product context positions GoStudio.ai as *"Your AI Studio for photos and videos — without the complexity"*, led by one-click tools and templates, with identity training as a supporting feature. It is built on two capabilities:

- Handpicked AI Models: identity-preserving generation for personal branding (photos + video) and product photography and catalog visuals.
- One-Click AI Tools: AI tools for editing, enhancement, generation, and video.

## Aim and Vision

The PDF states the aim as making high-quality professional headshots accessible through AI technology.

The PDF states the vision as becoming a leading provider of customizable AI-generated headshots and transforming how people represent themselves professionally online.

## Logo Design

The logo system includes:

- Logomark
- Logotype
- Lockup
- Clear space
- Minimum sizes
- Symbol variations
- Common mistakes
- Placement

### Logomark

The logomark symbolizes precision, innovation, and integration of contrasting elements. The connected parts represent a holistic approach.

### Logotype

The PDF names Work Sans Medium as the logotype font for GoStudio.ai. The logotype should feel modern, clean, balanced, recognizable, authentic, and innovative.

### Logo Lockup

The lockup combines the logomark and logotype. Use the lockup consistently to keep the brand recognizable across applications.

### Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logomark and logotype so the logo remains recognizable and uncluttered.

### Minimum Size

Minimum size from the PDF:

- Print: minimum height `.75"` / `.72"` as shown in the PDF extraction.
- Digital: minimum height `50px`.

Use at least `50px` height for digital logo applications unless the original visual PDF shows a more specific constraint for that exact use.

### Symbol Variations

The PDF includes symbol variations that may be used as brand symbols. When exact symbol selection matters, inspect the PDF visual pages directly.

PDF p.13 shows six approved symbols: the white "go" glyph with a dark long-shadow on a **square**, a **rounded square**, or a **circle**, each in a **gradient** or a **grey** fill.

### Logo Versions

PDF p.18 (Combinations) shows three lockup versions:

- **Positive:** gradient symbol + `#2A2A2A` wordmark, on white.
- **Monochrome:** grey symbol + white wordmark, on black.
- **Gradient:** gradient symbol + gradient-filled wordmark, on white.

### Common Logo Mistakes

Avoid:

- Adjusting logo elements.
- Rearranging logo elements.
- Distorting the logomark, logotype, or lockup.
- Using the wrong version for the background.

PDF p.14 illustrates six specific mistakes: squeezed letter spacing, rotating the lockup, a light-grey wordmark, stacking the symbol above the wordmark, adding a drop shadow, and shrinking the gap between symbol and wordmark.

### Logo Placement

Use the main positive logo version on light backgrounds.

Use the negative or gradient version on dark backgrounds where contrast supports legibility.

If a background image is too complex and makes the logo hard to read, use a different image or apply a treatment that restores legibility.

On photography (PDF p.15), the logo sits small in the **bottom-right corner**, using the white-wordmark version on dark photos. The "wrong" examples use the dark wordmark on dark photos, where it disappears.

## Colors

The PDF states that consistent color use is vital for brand recognition. The brand should be represented with the approved palette and combinations.

### Palette

The PDF lists these colors:

| Name | RGB (PDF) | HEX | CMYK (PDF) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White | 255, 255, 255 | `#FFFFFF` | 0, 0, 0, 0 | Light backgrounds and logo contrast. |
| Black / Dark | 42, 42, 42 | `#2A2A2A` | 0, 0, 0, 0.84 | Primary dark/text color. |
| Blue | 65, 36, 255 | `#4124FF` | 0.75, 0.86, 0, 0 | Saturated accent. Seen as a pill-tag fill ("#Healthcare") in collateral. |
| Purple | 160, 119, 254 | `#A077FE` | 0.37, 0.53, 0, 0 | Gradient start (left). |
| Scooter / Cyan | 1, 199, 228 | `#01C7E4` | 0.49, 0.56, 0, 0 | Gradient end (right). |

HEX values for Blue, Purple and Scooter are computed from the PDF's RGB values; the PDF prints HEX only for White and Black. The Scooter swatch on PDF p.17 renders periwinkle, which does not match its printed RGB (or CMYK); the gradient's cyan end confirms the RGB value. Treat RGB as authoritative.

### Gradient

The brand gradient runs **horizontally from Purple `#A077FE` (left) to Scooter `#01C7E4` (right)**, with 20%, 40%, 60%, and 80% tints shown on PDF p.17. In the brand assets it appears:

- inside the logomark circle (behind the white "go" glyph, with a dark long-shadow);
- as a thin bar along the bottom edge of social posts (PDF p.24);
- as gradient-filled highlight text in headers (PDF p.26: "Gostudioai.", the quoted tagline).

### Tints

The PDF shows color tint levels:

- 20%
- 40%
- 60%
- 80%

### Color Combinations

Use approved colors consistently. Avoid dominant off-brand colors unless specifically required by a page design and supported by the brand guideline visuals.

For audits:

- Flag primary colors outside the approved palette when used as dominant brand colors.
- Confirm contrast and logo legibility on backgrounds.
- Use the PDF visual page as the final source for gradients and exact visual combinations.

## Known Conflicts in the PDF

Recorded so they are reported rather than silently resolved (see Audit Priority):

1. **Banned words in PDF prose.** The intro (p.1, "empower"), aim (p.4, "democratize"), and logomark text (p.8, "seamless") use words banned by the product marketing context, as does the collateral line "Empowering people worldwide to elevate their brand." Do not reuse them in live copy.
2. **Stale positioning.** About, aim and vision (pp.3–4) describe a headshots-only product. The product marketing context supersedes them.
3. **Minimum print size.** The text says `.72"`; the graphic label says `.75"`. Use `.75"` (the larger) until confirmed.
4. **Gradient logo background.** p.15 says the gradient version goes on dark backgrounds; p.18 shows the gradient version on white.
5. **Type-scale labels.** p.22 labels 64px "Heading 1", 48px "Heading 2", then 36px and 24px both "Heading 1". Read them as H1 to H4 by size.
6. **Logotype typeface.** p.9 names Work Sans Medium and shows "Go Studio.ai" in it, but the lockup wordmark ("Studio.ai" beside the "go" symbol) on pp.8–18 is a heavier, tighter face. Use the supplied lockup artwork; never retype the wordmark.
